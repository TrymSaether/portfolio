# Stochastic and Statistical Models

**Stochastic and Statistical Models** is a long-form book project on probability, stochastic modeling, statistical structure, and mathematical models for uncertainty.

The project belongs to the same library layer as the numerical mathematics book: a manuscript-scale attempt to organize a large mathematical subject into a coherent text rather than a loose collection of isolated notes.

## Motivation

Uncertainty is not just noise added after the real mathematics is finished. In stochastic and statistical modeling, uncertainty is part of the structure: it changes what can be predicted, estimated, simulated, and explained.

This manuscript is intended to develop that structure carefully, moving between probability, random variables, statistical models, stochastic processes, inference, and model interpretation.

## Focus

The project is centered on the mathematical language of uncertainty:

- **Probability foundations** — events, random variables, distributions, expectation, variance, and conditional structure.
- **Statistical models** — parametric families, likelihood, estimation, uncertainty quantification, and model assumptions.
- **Stochastic processes** — time-indexed randomness, dependence, transition behavior, and dynamical uncertainty.
- **Model interpretation** — what a model says, what it assumes, and where its predictions become fragile.
